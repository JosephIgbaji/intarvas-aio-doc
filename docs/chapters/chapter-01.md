You can follow the steps below to access the system without any problems:

## 1. Log In to the System

1. **Address**: Go to the address `app.intarvas.com` from your browser.
2. **Information**: Log in by entering your username and password in the relevant fields.
3. **Verification**: Enter the verification code sent to your email address for your security and click the **"Log In"** button.

::: tip Small Tip
Verification codes sent to your email address may sometimes be delayed by a few minutes. If you do not receive the code, it will be helpful to wait for a short while before using the "Resend" option and to check the "Unwanted/Spam" folder.
:::

## 2. Password Reset (I Forgot My Password)

<div class="ref-url-badge">🔗 Access URL: https://app.intarvas.com/forgot-password</div>

If you do not remember your password, you can use the password reset section on the login page:

1. **Request**: Request a link by writing your email address in the password reset area.
2. **Email**: Click on the password reset link that has been sent to your inbox (or spam folder).
3. **Update**: Complete the transaction by creating a new, strong password on the opened page.
