🎧 How Does the Interviews Module Work?

All your interactions are collected and managed under the Conversations tab, regardless of their source.

## 🔄 Source of the Flow

On the Conversations screen, two basic types of operations are automatically opened in the form of "Person Card":

- **Communication Channels**: All messages received via web chat, WhatsApp, Social Media (Instagram, Facebook, etc.).
- **Phone Calls**: All incoming and outgoing voice calls made through the call center.

## 🗂 Person Card Advantages

Thanks to these cards, which open as soon as the interview begins, you can do the following: See the customer's past communication history instantly. Take notes during the interview. Transfer the transaction to the relevant department or representative.

::: info Note
At the end of the interview, you can increase reporting efficiency by selecting the transaction result code (disposition) through these cards.
:::

The Calendar tab on INTARVAS AIO gathers your entire business plan and team interactions on a single screen.

## 🔍 Viewing Options

Access URL; https://app.intarvas.com/calendar You can track at three basic levels through the calendar module:

- **Personal Calendar**: Your own appointments, tasks and reminders.
- **Shared Calendars**: Private work plans shared with your team members or managers.
- **General (All) Calendar**: All events, meetings or department-based planning throughout the company.

## 🛠 What Can You Do?

- **Planning**: You can invite participants by creating a new event or meeting.
- **Synchronization**: Thanks to shared calendars, you can see the availability of your team members instantly.
- **Filtering**: You can only focus on your own plans or analyze the intensity of the entire team in a single view.

::: tip A Small Tip
Appointments taken through the person cards opened in the Meetings module are automatically processed into this Calendar section, ensuring that you do not miss any follow-up.
:::
