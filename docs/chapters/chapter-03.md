📧 Inbox Module

Access URL; https://app.intarvas.com/inbox The Inbox on INTARVAS AIO allows you to manage all your correspondence from within the platform without the need for an external email client.

## 📥 Basic Operations

You can quickly perform the following operations via your Inbox:

- **Check**: You can instantly list all incoming emails and mark them as read/unread.
- **Do not reply**: You can reply directly to emails from your customers or business partners through the platform.
- **Forward**: You can forward an email that reaches you to other team members about the subject or to a different address.

## 💡 Integration Advantage

The emails in the incoming box are not just messages; they are also records: If the sender in the incoming email is registered in the system, it is automatically associated with the relevant Person Card. Thus, you can see email traf fi c with past phone calls or chat records on the same screen.

::: info Important Note
When replying to your emails, you can create a standard and professional communication language by using the ready-made Draft Texts on the platform.
:::
