✅ Task Management Module

Access URL; https://app.intarvas.com/tasks Task Management is a central control point that allows you to organize your projects and daily tasks, and assign responsibilities to your team members.

## 🛠 Basic Features and Usage

You can manage your work flow through this module with the following steps:

- **Job Assignment**: You can directly assign a specific task (task) to other users or teams on the platform.
- **Detailed**: You can clarify the scope of the work by adding an explanation, file attachment, priority status and deadline (due date) to the tasks.
- **Tracking and Monitoring**: You can instantly monitor which stage your assigned tasks are in (to be done, ongoing, completed).
- **Feedback**: You can quickly communicate about the process by commenting on the tasks.

## 🔗 Integration with Other Modules

Task Management also talks to the other parts of INTARVAS AIO: A note taken during a Meeting can be quickly converted into an Assignment. The end date of the task is automatically processed into the user's Calendar.

::: tip Professional Tip
By marking your tasks as "High Priority", you can ensure that your team focuses on the most critical tasks and minimize delays.
:::
