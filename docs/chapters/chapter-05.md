📚 Information Bank Module

Information Bank is a module that enables you to digitize the knowledge base of your institution and allows your users to access the information they need as quickly as possible.

## 🛠 Basic Operations and Configuration

You can organize your information through this module as follows:

- **Creating Categories**: You can create a regular structure by dividing your information into main categories such as "Technical Support", "Sales Processes", "IK Policies" or "Product Guides".
- **Title and Content**: Under each category, you can open specific topics in headings; you can add detailed texts, images or documents to these headings.
- **Quick Access**: You can ensure that your users (customer representatives or field teams) can reach the correct guide within seconds to solve a problem they encounter.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Shortens the Training Period**: New users provide rapid adaptation with the ready- made guides on the platform.
- **Reduces Error Rate**: Since the standard steps of all processes are recorded in the Information Bank, transactions are carried out without errors.
- **Self-Service Support**: If this bank opens up to the outside world, your customers can solve their own problems without being connected to a representative.

::: tip Professional Advice
By regularly updating the Knowledge Base, you can ensure that your team always acts according to the most accurate and up-to-date procedures.
:::
