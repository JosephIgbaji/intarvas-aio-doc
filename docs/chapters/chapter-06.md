## ⚡ Quick Add Module

Quick Add is a time-saving "shortcut" center located in the upper right corner of the platform or at an easily accessible point. You can start the following operations instantly with a single click:

## 🛠 What Can You Do?

- **Add Person**: You can quickly manually register a new customer or contact to the system without going to the detailed guide page.
- **Company Addition**: You can create new institution and organization records for your B2B processes in seconds.
- **Creating a Request**: During or after a conversation, you can open a new support/work request (ticket) that needs to be followed up.
- **Sending Email**: You can send emails directly by opening a new email draft without having to switch to the "Inbox" module.

## 💡 Usage Advantage

The biggest benefit of this module is that it provides uninterrupted working opportunities. For example; While planning in your Calendar, you do not need to leave your current screen to save a customer that comes to mind or to create an emergency email draft.

::: tip Professional Recommendation
When adding manual people, entering all email and phone information ensures that the Interviews to be conducted in the future are automatically matched with the correct "Person Card". One of the most important features that completes the structure of the INTARVAS AIO platform in global standards and its user-friendly interface is Language Options. This feature allows the personalization of the platform for teams that provide services in different languages.
