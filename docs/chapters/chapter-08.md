## 🌓 View Options

The appearance allows you to personalize your working environment and adapt to different lighting conditions. Two basic mode options are offered in the INTARVAS AIO interface:

## 🌗 Offered Modes

- **Day Mode (Light Mode)**: Ideal for a standard, bright and spacious working environment. It allows text to be read more clearly in high-light environments.
- **Dark Mode**: Reduces eye strain in low-light environments. While providing an aesthetic appearance, it also contributes to the battery life of devices (especially mobile devices).

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Eye Health**: In prolonged use, Dark Mode can be preferred to reduce the effect of blue light, which can strain the eyes.
- **Focus**: Allowing users to adjust the interface according to their own visual preferences positively affects their work motivation and screen focus time.

::: tip Professional Advice
You can switch between modes at any time from your profile settings and update the appearance according to the time of day.
:::
