## 🔔 Notification Module

Notifications are a central communication point that allows you to track your work flows on INTARVAS AIO in real time. The system automatically alerts you when critical events occur.

## 🛠 Notification Types

You will receive instant notifications in the following categories through the system:

- **Reminders**: Timely alerts for events you add to your calendar or upcoming important appointments.
- **Request Notifications**: New job requests (tickets), assignments assigned to you, or status updates for your current requests.
- **Evaluations**: Customer satisfaction surveys, performance feedbacks or analysis outputs generated by the system.
- **System Announcements**: Platform updates or important internal announcements.

## 💡 Usage Advantage

- **Unmissable Tasks**: You don't need to constantly check your screen; the system will alert you when a new task comes in.
- **Fast Reaction**: It increases service quality by allowing you to respond instantly to customer requests or urgent tasks.
- **Regular Work flow**: Thanks to reminders, you will never miss any meeting or deadline during the day.

::: tip Professional Advice
By marking as "Read" from the notification panel, you can focus only on the current notifications that you are not interested in yet and keep your panel organized.
:::
