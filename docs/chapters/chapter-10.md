## 🔍 Search Module

Search is a central tool that allows you to access all kinds of data registered in the INTARVAS AIO database in the fastest way. It enables you to work target-oriented without getting lost among complex menus.

## 🛠 What Can You Find?

You can quickly search between the records below using the search bar:

- **Customer Records**: Reach people registered with their name, surname, phone number or email address instantly.
- **Company Information**: Find your corporate customers or business partners in your B2B processes by name.
- **Requests and Negotiations**: Go to the relevant record by calling the reference number of a request or correspondence opened in the past.

## 💡 Usage Advantage

- **Time Savings**: Instead of manual listing, you reach the result directly with a single keyword.
- **Duplicate Registration Prevention**: Before adding a new person, you can check if that person is already registered in the system by searching.
- **Quick Action**: As soon as you fi nd your customer, you can start a new "Interview" or assign them a "Task".

::: tip Professional Tip
While searching, you can further narrow down the results by using specific details such as the last 4 digits of the phone number or the email extension, not just the name.
:::
