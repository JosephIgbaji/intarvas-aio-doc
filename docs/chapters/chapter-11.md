## 🟢 Status Management

The status allows you to determine your immediate availability level on the platform. This way, your team members and managers can easily see if you are currently working.

## 🛠 How Do You Update Your Status?

You can press the following options from the status menu located at the top of the platform or in the profile area:

- ✅ **Ready/Active**: Indicates that you are ready to answer incoming calls, correspondence and tasks.
- ☕ **Break**: Used during short rest intervals.
- 🍽 **Meal Break**: It updates your status during the daily meal time.
- 👥 **Meeting**: Indicates that you are in an internal or external discussion, and that you cannot focus on a new job at that moment.
- ⚙ **Special Situations**: You can also choose other options such as "Training", "Technical Issue" or "Project Work" determined by your institution.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Correct Job Distribution**: The system cannot automatically assign a new live call (Call/Chat) to a user who is in the "Break" or "Meeting" mode.
- **Team Transparency**: Before reaching a friend, you can instantly understand if they are available by checking their status.
- **Efficient Reporting**: At the end of the day, the amount of time spent on each situation can be reported, which strengthens operational planning.

::: tip Professional Advice
Not forgetting to update your status when leaving your desk prevents customers from being kept waiting and ensures that intra-team communication runs smoothly.
:::
