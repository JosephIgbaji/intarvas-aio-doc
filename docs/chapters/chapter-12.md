## 👤 My Profile Module

Access URL; https://app.intarvas.com/settings/profile My Profile is the section where you manage your personal information on the platform, update your security and end your session.

## 🛠 What Can You Do?

- **Changing Profile Information**: You can update your personal identi fi ers such as your name, surname, contact information or profile photo.
- **Password Update**: To protect your account security, you can periodically or when needed, change your current password to a new one.
- **Log-out**: You can perform the secure log-out operation to ensure the security of your account when your work is over or when you disconnect from a shared device.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Security**: Updating your password from this menu at regular intervals minimizes the risk of unauthorized access.
- **Representation**: Having up-to-date profile information ensures that you appear with the correct identity in internal communications and customer contact.
- **Data Privacy**: Using the "Log Out" option helps protect your data by preventing your session from remaining open.

::: warning Security Note
When updating your password; it is recommended that you choose complex combinations containing uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters for your system security.
:::
