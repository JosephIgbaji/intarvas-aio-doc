## 🏗 Workplace (Quick Access Panel)

Workplace is a multifunctional control center that accelerates your INTARVAS AIO experience, accessible by tapping the square button in the lower right corner of every screen. It contains four main tools: 1. 📞 Call Screen (Softphone)

- **Manual Search**: You can start an instant voice call by dialing the number you want to call.
- **Call History**: You can list all your recent calls, incoming, outgoing and unanswered, and make a return with one click.
- **Calling with Different Numbers**: Within your authorization, you can make calls by selecting different outgoing numbers (DIDs) de fi ned in the system. 2. ✅ To-Do List (To-Do List)
- **Full Integration**: This list works in sync with the "To Do" section on your homepage.
- **Quick Follow-up**: While having a conversation or reading an email, you can add a new task or highlight the tasks you have completed.

## 💬

### 3. 💬 Chat (Messenger)

- **Personal Communication**: You can instantly write to other users, call them directly by voice, record voice messages or send important documents.
- **Group Chats**: You can provide file sharing and fast coordination by creating team or department-based groups. 4. 📇 Phone Directory
- **Person Management**: You can quickly register new people in the guide.
- **Detailed Information**: Not only the number; you can also store the person's location and additional contact information in the form of a business card.

::: tip Professional Advice
Since the Workplace panel is in a "collapsible" structure, you can close it when you are done with your work and expand your screen area, and you can continue from where you left off with just one click when you need it.
:::

## 💬 Conversation (Interview Management) Module

Conversation allows you to manage message traf fi c from all connected communication channels (WhatsApp, Web Chat, Social Media, etc.) from a single screen without any confusion.

## 🛠 Advanced Filtering and Editing Options

You can use the following tools to manage your meeting list efficiently:

- **Channel-Based Filtering**: You can quickly filter between channels to focus only on a specific source (e.g., only WhatsApp or only Web Chat).
- **Account-Based Filtering**: If you have multiple accounts belonging to the same channel type (for example, two different Instagram accounts), you can narrow down the conversations by selecting between these accounts.
- **Smart Sorting**: You can sort the meetings chronologically in "Oldest to Newest" or "Newest to Oldest" according to your business priorities.
- **Mute**: When you need to focus during heavy traf fi c or for a certain period of time, you can prevent notification clutter by muting the channels you choose.

## 💡 Usage Advantage

- **Focused Work**: Thanks to filters, you only see messages on the channels you are responsible for, and you prevent distractions.
- **Quick Answer**: You can instantly react to the most recent messages by reordering them in reverse order.
- **Flexibility**: The mute channels feature helps representatives manage screen clutter in situations such as breaks or meetings.

::: tip Professional Recommendation
You can capture the most fresh customer requests using the "Back to Old" ranking during the busiest hours of the day, and you can clean all past requests waiting with the "Old to New" ranking during off-peak hours.
:::
