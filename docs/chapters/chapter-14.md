The listing structure on the meeting screen is divided into two basic categories to distribute the workload fairly and regularly:

## 📥 1. Queued Items (Queue)

This tab includes all interactions that have just been added to the system and have not yet been answered by a customer representative:

- **Source**: Every new message coming from communication channels (WhatsApp, Web Chat, etc.) first falls here.
- **Ownership**: There is no owner yet. Representatives start processing the negotiations "on behalf of" this pool.
- **Speed**: It is critical to keep the waiting time in the queue to a minimum for customer satisfaction.

## 👤 2. Online (Active Conversations)

This tab represents meetings owned and actively managed by a representative:

- **Under Processing**: All transactions that the user (representative) has taken over and continues to communicate with are listed here.
- **Management**: The representative responds to the customer, sends the file or concludes the interview through this tab.
- **Personal Pool**: Each representative keeps his focus by following the "Online" meetings he takes on himself.

::: tip Professional Advice
When you take a waiting appointment in line, the system automatically opens the customer's Personal Card; thus, you can start the appointment with full control.
:::
