# Chapter 16: 🚀 Interview Start Module

Start Meeting is a tool that allows you to get out of passive waiting and start an active communication process. When you want to reach the customer, you can engage in a dialogue by selecting the desired channel through this module.

## 🛠 How to Use?

- **Channel Selection**: You can select the platform you want to contact (WhatsApp, SMS, Email, etc.) from the list.
- **Person Identi fi cation**: You can select a registered person in your guide or specify a new target by entering a number/email.
- **Starting the Transaction**: A call window will open through the channel you have selected and you can send your first message/call.

## 💡 Usage Advantage

- **Proactive Support**: When a problem is solved or you want to provide information about a campaign, you can take the first step for the customer.
- **Channel Flexibility**: You can increase your access rate by selecting the channel that the customer uses or prefers the most (e.g., only WhatsApp).
- **Central Registration**: Every conversation you initiate automatically creates a "Meeting Record" in the system and is processed into the past.

::: tip Professional Recommendation
When starting a conversation, you can send your messages faster and in a more corporate language by using the ready-made text templates in the Knowledge Base.
:::
