Every interaction on the INTARVAS AIO platform has a purpose and a result. Processing this result into the system when the interview is completed is essential for the health of the operation.

## 🛠 Why Should You Choose a Result Code?

- **Reporting**: It allows you to see numerically how many meetings were held on which topics at the end of the day (e.g. Sales, Technical Support, Complaints, etc.).
- **History Control**: When browsing backwards from the "History" tab, it helps you understand what happened by just looking at the code without listening to or reading the content of the conversation.
- **Transaction Tracking**: By analyzing which topics are experiencing high intensity, you can shape company strategies according to these data.

## 🔄 How Does the Process Work?

- **Automatic Card**: For every call made (Incoming/Outgoing Call, WhatsApp, Chat, etc.), the system automatically opens a Person Card.
- **Closing Stage**: When the interview ends, a list of "Result Codes" appears in front of the representative.
- **Mandatory Choice**: You must end the conversation by selecting the code that best matches the nature of the transaction.

::: info Important Note
Person cards that are closed or left un fi nished without selecting the result code will appear as "Unknown" in reports, negatively affecting efficiency analyses. Making this selection after each interview is an integral part of a professional work flow.
:::
