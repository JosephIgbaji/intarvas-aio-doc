Choosing a result code in INTARVAS AIO is not just a label; it can trigger complex work flows running in the background.

## 🛠 Mandatory Areas and Integrations

You can define the following "mandatory" actions behind the strategic result codes you have determined:

- **Reminder & Calendar**: Depending on the selected code, the system may set up an automatic reminder for the user or open a date field in the calendar.
- **Form & Agreement**: If the meeting ends with a sale or contract, it may be mandatory to fill out the relevant form or agreement text.

## ✉ Automatic SMS and Email Sending

Thanks to the "Template Triggerer", one of the most efficient features, the process works as follows:

- **Definition**: Ready SMS or Email templates are attached behind certain result codes (e.g., "Information Provided", "Appointment Approved").
- **Trigger**: The system activates as soon as the user fi nishes the consultation and selects the relevant result code.
- **Automatic Sending**: The notification message defined for the customer (the person who received the interview) is automatically sent in the second when the personal card is closed.

## 💡 Usage Advantage

- **Speed**: Eliminates the burden of the representative manually writing emails or SMS.
- **Standard**: Ensures that all customers receive an error-free response in the same professional language.
- **Complete Data**: Thanks to mandatory fields, it guarantees that no form or appointment is missed.

::: tip Professional Tip
By attaching a "Thank You Email" to critical codes such as "Sale Completed", you can take the customer experience (CX) to the highest level without the need for human intervention.
:::
