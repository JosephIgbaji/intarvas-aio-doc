Protocol) When a meeting or transaction is over, manually closing the Person Card you are working on is the most critical step for the accuracy of your data.

## 🛠 Transaction Steps and Critical Rules

- **Manual Close**: After making all your note entries, result code selections and adjustments, you must end the card using the "Save and Close" button.
- **Data Approval**: This process ensures that the changes you make are permanently processed into the database. ⚠ 24 Hour Rule (Automatic Shutdown) If a person leaves the card open, a security mechanism applied by the system to protect the data health comes into play:
- **Duration**: If the period expires within 24 hours from the moment the card is opened, the system card will be automatically closed.
- **Result**: Cards that are automatically closed are considered terminated without selecting the result code.
- **Impact**: This situation causes the occurrence of "Uncertain/Incomplete Data" in reports and leads to the erroneous measurement of representative performance.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Instant Reporting**: As soon as you close the card, the data for that meeting is re fl ected in the management panel and statistics.
- **Workload Management**: Open cards accumulate on the representative's screen and make it dif fi cult to focus.
- **System Health**: Manual shutdown ensures that automation (SMS/Email sending) works correctly by ensuring that you meet the "Result Code" requirement.

::: tip Professional Advice
Before your shift ends, make sure to check all your open person cards and click "Save and Close". Cards that are stuck in the 24-hour rule may bring up the question of "why it did not result" in past checks.
:::
