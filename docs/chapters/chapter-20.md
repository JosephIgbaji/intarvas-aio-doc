# Chapter 20: 📋 Personal Card and Data Management

The Person Card, which opens when an interaction begins, is built on three main pillars to identify the customer and manage the process correctly:

## 🛠 1. Contact Information (Contact Info)

You can centrally keep all transportation channels related to the customer in this area:

- **Identity**: The person's full name and surname.
- **Phone World**: You can enter more than one phone number belonging to a person into the system, not just one number.
- **Digital Addresses**: By adding one or more email addresses of the person, you can match the correspondence history with these addresses.

## 👤 2. Owner of the Second Meeting (Ownership)

There is a ownership structure in the system that operates according to the principles of "responsibility" and "tracking":

- **First Contact Rule**: The representative who initiates the meeting or provides the first communication by taking the queue is automatically assigned as the "Owner" of that meeting by the system.
- **Liability**: This structure clearly shows who the recipient is when there is a development related to that customer in the future.

## 🏢 3. Company Relationship Linkage (B2B Integration)

You can connect individual customers with corporate structures:

- **Corporate Relationship**: If there is an institution/company that the person works for or is af fi liated with, you can add this information to the person card.
- **Centralized View**: Thus, you can report all meetings made by different people from the same company under a single corporate umbrella.

::: tip Professional Advice
The more detailed (such as multiple emails and phone numbers) you fi ll in the information on your personal card, the higher the likelihood that requests from different channels in the future will be matched with the same person, and duplicate registrations are prevented.
:::
