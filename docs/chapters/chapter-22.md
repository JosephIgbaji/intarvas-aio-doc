# Chapter 22: 🚫 Block (Block Person and Channel)

</div>

Block is a security tool used to restrict the access of a specific person or number to your system. This way, your teams can focus only on real customer requests.

## 🛠 How to Apply and What are the Options?

When you decide to block a person, the following flexible options appear:

- **Channel-Based Selection**: You can specifically choose which platforms (WhatsApp, SMS, Voice Call, etc.) a person will be blocked from using. If you wish, you can block access from only a specific channel or all communication channels.
- **Add Explanation**: You can note the reason for the blocking process (e.g., "Spam content", "Incorrect number", "Improper tone") as a note to the system.
- **Permanent/Temporary Solution**: This process completely prevents that person from wasting time by falling on the screen of the representatives.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Efficiency**: Prevents spam (unnecessary) calls or messages from fi lling the "Queue" structure and causing real customers to wait.
- **Representative Motivation**: It maintains work peace by cutting off access to the system for individuals who exhibit inappropriate behavior.
- **Transparency**: Thanks to the statements written, your other team members can also see why the person in question was blocked from their past records.

::: tip Professional Recommendation
Filling out the "Description" section before blocking guides the management in future inspections or in the detection of blocked items made by mistake.
:::
