# Chapter 24: 📋 More Information (Segmentation and Title)

More Information allows you to detail your customer's profile beyond the basic contact information on the person card. This field is especially critical for marketing and correct targeting strategies.

## 🛠 What can you de fi ne?

You can process the following data using the title and category fields under this tab:

- **Customer Type**: You can determine service priority by categorizing your customer into categories such as "Potential", "Loyal Customer", "Supplier" or "VIP".
- **Professional Group**: By selecting the person's field of expertise (e.g., Doctor, Engineer, Lawyer, etc.), you create a basis for offering them special campaigns or information.
- **Title Selection**: You can determine how to address the person during the interview or their position in the corporate hierarchy (e.g., General Manager, Operations Manager, etc.) from here.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Personalized Experience**: Knowing your customer's profession or title allows you to establish a more professional and personalized communication language with them.
- **Targeted Reporting**: It helps you get answers to specific questions such as "How many engineers are registered in our system?" or "How many meetings were held with our VIP customers this month?"
- **Segmentation**: It facilitates sales or marketing teams to go to the right professional groups with the right offers.

::: tip Professional Recommendation
If you are managing a B2B (intercompany) process, fi lling in the "Title" section allows you to quickly identify the decision- maker within the company (purchase manager, etc.).
:::
