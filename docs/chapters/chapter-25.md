# Chapter 25: 📍 Address Information and Map Integration

Address Information allows you to record your customer's physical location on the personal card to the fi nest detail and visualize this location instantly.

## 🛠 Features and Usage

- **Detailed Registration**: You can process all address components such as country, city, district, neighborhood, street and door number in separate fields or in the description section in detail.
- **Smart Map Zoom In**: When you start typing the address or enter the full address, the system processes this data in the background and automatically zooms in the map panel on the side to that location.
- **Veri fi cation**: Thanks to the marking on the map, you can visually con fi rm the accuracy of the address you entered instantly.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Logistics and Field Management**: If you provide technical service, courier or on-site support service, it allows your staff to transfer accurate location information.
- **Quick Action**: Seeing the customer's location on the map makes it easier to locate the nearest field team or dealer.
- **Regional Analysis**: You can develop regional marketing strategies by seeing the locations where your customers are concentrated.

::: tip Professional Tip
When writing the address, don't forget to add additional information such as the building name or "type". Thanks to the map integration, you not only write an address, but you also digitally mark the route to your customer's door.
:::
