# Chapter 26: 📱 Social Media Profiles and Integration

Social Media allows you to collect your customer's digital footprint in the digital world (username and profile links) on the personal card at one point.

## 🛠 What can you de fi ne?

You can process the user information belonging to the following platforms that your customer actively uses into the system:

- **Corporate and Individual Networks**: User addresses on platforms such as LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Instagram, Facebook.
- **Direct Link**: By saving user names in this field, you enable your team to better familiarize the customer with the digital environment.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

360 Degree Customer View: Knowing which platforms your customer is active on increases your chances of reaching them through the most accurate channel.

- **Communication Speed**: When responding to a request received via social media, the fact that this information about the person's profile is recorded on their card ensures that the representative is speaking with the right person.
- **Marketing and Brand Perception**: By analyzing your customers' social media habits, you can provide them with a more personalized experience.

::: tip Professional Recommendation
Especially in B2B processes, adding your customer's LinkedIn profile to this area makes it easier for you to make a short preliminary preparation before the interview and to communicate more effectively.
:::
