(Custom Fields)

Additional Information allows you to design special data entry fields that your institution needs when standard data fields ( first name, last name, phone, etc.) are not suf fi cient. This section is managed under the Customization menu of the platform. 🧩 Form Elements and Options You can add these different data entry types according to your needs:

- **Text Boxes (Text Box)**: For entering free notes or special codes. Single Selection (Radio Button / Dropdown): For situations where you want only one option to be selected from a specific list.
- **Multiple Selection (Checkbox)**: Areas where multiple features or interests can be checked at the same time.
- **Date and Time Selectors**: For special reminders or event dates. 🔒 Flexibility and Obligation
- **Dynamic Structure**: You can open as much optional space as you want, and you can update these spaces as your business processes change.
- **Required Fields**: You can make any field you want "required to be fi lled" in order to ensure that critical data is not skipped. This way, the "Save" or "Select Result Code" operation cannot be performed without that field being fi lled.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Industry Compliance**: It integrates sector-specific data such as "Property Type" for a real estate company and "Device Serial Number" for a technical service into the system.
- **Data Standard**: Thanks to the form elements, it prevents chaos by ensuring that all representatives enter data in the same format.
- **Advanced Reporting**: By filtering through these customized areas, you can obtain much more detailed and in-depth operational analyses.

::: tip Professional Advice
Opening too many mandatory fields can slow down the representative's speed. Therefore, only making the fields that are "essential" for your reporting mandatory and leaving the others optional increases operational efficiency.
:::
