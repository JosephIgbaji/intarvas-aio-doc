Contact Information is the center of all static and dynamic data recorded in the system about a customer or partner. This area allows the representative to see all kinds of details he may need at the moment of the interview at a glance.

## 🛠 What is Included in the Module?

- **Basic Personal Information**: First and last name, main phone numbers and primary email addresses.
- **Location Information (Address)**: Location detection with physical location details and map integration.
- **Social Media Identities**: Usernames on digital platforms such as Instagram, X, LinkedIn.
- **Corporate Identity (Title)**: The person's occupational group, position within the company and way of addressing. Private Areas (Additional Information): Customized according to your institution's work flow; text boxes, selectable forms and mandatory data entry fields.

## 💡

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Integrated View**: It allows the representative to access all critical data about the customer from a single screen without having to navigate between different tabs.
- **Consistency**: Thanks to the special areas you add to the "Additional Information" section, it ensures that all your team members collect information at the same standard.
- **Personalization**: Details such as social media and title increase the quality of the approach established to the customer.

::: tip Professional Tip
Think of the contact information section as a "living entity". Including a social media account or an updated title that is missing in every meeting will increase the value of your database day by day.
:::
