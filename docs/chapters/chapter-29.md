# Chapter 29: 🕒 Past (Interview Timeline)

History is like a diary listing all the touchpoints (touchpoints) between that customer and your institution in the person card. A representative can have an overview of the customer's entire journey by looking at this section before starting the interview.

## 🔍 What Can Be Seen in the History Tab?

- **Channel Information**: Which platform the interview took place on (WhatsApp, Voice Call, Email, SMS, etc.).
- **Representative Information**: Which of your team members/representatives has previously spoken to the customer (Accountability tracking).
- **Result Codes**: How previous conversations ended (e.g., "Complaint Solved", "Offer Pending", "Information Provided").
- **Time Stamp**: Information about the day and time the interview started/ended. Content Access: (If authorized) Quick access to old correspondence content or voice recordings.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Continuity**: It allows the representative to continue from where he left off without the customer having to say "I told you this before".
- **Context Provides**: If the customer's last 3 consultations ended with a "Complaint" code, the representative may adopt a more empathetic approach in this consultation.
- **Performance Analysis**: It is an unique source to track how many different representatives a request goes to and how many steps it is solved in.

::: tip Professional Tip
Before starting to talk when you receive a new call, quickly glancing at the "History" tab allows you to gain trust by making the customer feel that you know their previous problems, beyond addressing them by their name.
:::
