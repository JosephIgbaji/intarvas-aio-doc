# Chapter 31: 📝 Note (Customer Special Notes)

Note is the area where you record special information about that customer that only you or your team can see, which is included in the person card. Unlike the interview texts, this is used for strategic and reminder information.

## 🛠 What Can Be Noted?

- **Critical Information**: Operational warnings such as "The customer only wants to be called in the afternoon", "He is a user with VIP status", "He may repeat the previous technical problem".
- **Personal Details**: Address and communication preferences such as "Mr. X should be consulted with the brain assistant", "He prefers this template in corporate correspondence".
- **Process Information**: Additional notes that are not included in the interview but are necessary for the completion of the file.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Team Synchronization**: Your team member who will meet with that person after you can instantly see the customer's special sensitivities by looking at the notes section.
- **Quick Adaptation**: Instead of re-defining the customer every time, you can make him feel special with the small notes you receive.
- **Data Depth**: It increases service quality by recording "human" or "situational" details that are not included in forms or result codes.

::: tip Professional Advice
Keep notes short, concise, and understandable. Especially notes that start with phrases like "Important" or "Urgent" attract the representative's attention as soon as the card is opened, minimizing the margin of error.
:::
