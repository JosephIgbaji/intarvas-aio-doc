Tracking) A form is a module designed for you to obtain information from the customer within a certain logical framework during an interview or to review forms that the customer has previously filled out.

## 🛠 What Can Be Done with the Form Module?

- **Data Entry During the Interview**: While speaking with the customer (voice or written), you can fill out the form by asking the questions defined in the system in order. This creates a "question-answer" flow, ensuring that no detail is missed.
- **Viewing Customer Forms**: If your customer filled out a form from your website or another channel (e.g., Application form, Complaint form), this data is listed directly under the Forms tab.
- **Standard Information Flow**: It creates an institutional standard in data entry by ensuring that all representatives ask the same questions.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Reduces Error Rate**: The representative does not need to think about what to ask; he completes the process professionally by filling in the fields in the form.
- **Segmentation and Sales**: Depending on the answers from the forms, you can correctly segment the customer or instantly capture a sales opportunity.
- **Centralized Data**: The data entered by the customer himself and the data entered by the representative meet in the same place, thus preventing inconsistencies.

::: tip Professional Recommendation
When filling out a "Satisfaction Survey" or "Troubleshooting Log Form", processing the customer's answers on the form in real time eliminates the hassle of taking notes afterward and increases your productivity.
:::
