The product is the section in the personal card where you track which services or products your customer is interested in and what they are currently using.

## 🛠 What Can Be Done with the Product Module?

- **Adding Products**: You can link the products that the customer has contacted, purchased or wants to purchase to the personal card by selecting them from your portfolio.
- **Interest Level Tracking**: If a customer is interested in multiple products, you can see all these products in a list and determine your focus accordingly.
- **Capturing Sales Opportunity**: During the meeting, the representative adds a product mentioned by the customer here, creating valuable data for the sales team.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Targeted Campaign**: It allows you to make filters such as "Send SMS only to customers who are interested in product X".
- **Personalized Offer**: By instantly seeing which product you are talking about when a customer calls, you can make an effective introduction such as "We have an update about the X product you were interested in last week."
- **Strategic Reporting**: It allows you to analyze which products you receive the most calls about or which products are more interested in.

::: tip Professional Advice
Even if the customer only asked to "get information", add the product they are interested in to this section. This way, when there is a discount or campaign related to that product in the future, you will have a ready- made list to reach that customer.
:::
