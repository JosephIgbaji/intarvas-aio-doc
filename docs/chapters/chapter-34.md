# Chapter 34: 📁 Media (Image and File Archive)

Media is a pool where all files shared by the customer through communication channels such as WhatsApp, Email, Web Chat are automatically collected and displayed.

## 🛠 What Does the Media Module Cover?

Any kind of digital material received during correspondence is automatically recorded in this section:

- **Images**: Photos, screenshots (SS), product images.
- **Documents**: PDF files, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets.
- **Multimedia**: Voice recordings (Voice Message), videos.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Quick Access**: Instead of searching for a receipt or a malfunction photo in thousands of lines of correspondence history, you can access all files at a glance by clicking on the "Media" tab.
- **Data Integrity**: No evidence or document sent by the customer is lost in the system; it is permanently matched with the person card.
- **Operational Speed**: Especially in technical support or accounting processes, it allows you to instantly download and process the shared document.

::: tip Professional Advice
When reviewing a return or complaint process, first check the "Media" section. An image sent by the customer can provide a much more effective and faster solution to understanding the problem than a long text.
:::
