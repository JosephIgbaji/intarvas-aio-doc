# Chapter 35: ⏰ Reminders (Timed Returns)

Reminders are an alarm mechanism set up to remind you not to forget to return to the customer on a specific issue (giving a price quote, collecting information, calling back, etc.) after a meeting is over.

## 🔔 Notification System and Scheduling

The system does not leave you alone when the reminder time you have planned approaches and sends you a two-stage warning: 1 Hour Before: The first notification arrives; this is a warning to plan and prepare for the upcoming task (e.g., reviewing the file). 10 Minutes Before: The second and final notification arrives; this is an "instant" warning reminding you that you are about to proceed to the meeting.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Customer Loyalty**: A customer who is called at the time you promised increases the trust he has in your institution.
- **Operational Memory**: It takes the burden of remembering when to return to which customer at what time in the busy pace of the day off your shoulders.
- **Professional Tracking**: Thanks to notifications, a flawless work flow is ensured so that no return request is overlooked.

::: tip Professional Advice
If you close a conversation with the code "Will be called again", be sure to set up a Reminder at the same second. This way, the system will start the tracking process on your behalf and will alert you when the time comes.
:::
