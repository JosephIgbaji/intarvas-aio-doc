(PDF Proposal Submission)

The agreement provides you with a fi nancial basis for an agreement for the product or services a customer is interested in. This module turns the discussion into a verbal commitment into a written commercial offer.

## 🛠 Sales Process with Agreement Module

- **Creating an Agreement**: You can create a digital agreement draft containing price, quantity and discount details for the products the customer is interested in.
- **Automatic PDF Production**: The agreement details you prepare turn into a stylish and corporate PDF Proposal File in seconds.
- **Omnichannel Shipping**: You can send the created PDF offer directly to the customer with one click via WhatsApp, Email or other active communication channels.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Corporate Image**: Instead of writing a price by hand to the customer, offering a professional PDF offer from the system increases the reliability of your brand.
- **Speed and Efficiency**: You do not need to use external programs (Word, Excel, etc.) to prepare a proposal; everything starts and ends within INTARVAS AIO.
- **Centralized Tracking**: All sent offers remain tied to the personal card. Thus, even months later, the amount of the offer given to the customer can be viewed immediately.

::: tip Professional Advice
When you create an agreement, do not forget to specify the expiration date of your offer. After sending the PDF, you can set up a Reminder through the system and set an alarm for yourself to ask about the fate of the offer.
:::
