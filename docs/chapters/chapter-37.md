Details

The campaign is the section where you monitor bulk mailing, call lists or marketing operations that include your customer within the personal card.

## 🔍 What Can Be Seen with the Campaign Module?

- **Active Campaigns**: Which active campaign lists the customer's number is listed in (e.g., "Spring Discount Calls", "New Member Welcome SMS Group").
- **Campaign Status**: Information on whether the person has been reached within the scope of the campaign, whether the message has been sent or not, or whether the call has been made or not.
- **Operational Context**: The representative instantly understands why the customer was called or why the customer returned (based on which campaign message they called) thanks to this field.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Correct Information Flow**: When the customer says "I received a campaign message," the representative does not need to ask "Which campaign are you talking about?"; he sees the detail instantly on the card.
- **Prevents Repeated Communication**: The representative, seeing that the person is already on a campaign list, does not tire the customer by offering the same offer again.
- **Marketing Efficiency**: This is the most accurate data from the field to analyze which campaigns receive more feedback from which customer groups.

::: tip Professional Advice
If you see an active record in the "Campaign" tab when a call comes in, starting the conversation by reminding the customer of the benefits of this campaign will signi fi cantly increase your sales closing rate.
:::
