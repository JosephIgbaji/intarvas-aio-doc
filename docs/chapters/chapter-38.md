# Chapter 38: 📞 Manual Interviews (Offline Interaction Log)

Manual Interviews allows interactions that do not occur automatically via INTARVAS AIO (e.g., face-to-face interview, call made from an external phone, of fi ce visit, etc.) to be processed as a "Log" (record) on the person card.

## 🛠 How to Apply and What Does It Cover?

When a communication is provided other than through CRM, you can manually add this meeting to the system:

- **Interview Details**: You can add a short summary of where and how the interview was conducted.
- **Log Entry**: This entry is placed in the same chronological order as all other digital interactions (WhatsApp, Voice Call, etc.) in the "History" tab.
- **Continuity**: The representative keeps the corporate memory up to date by recording that the customer has come to the of fi ce or has been called while out.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Complete History**: All the customer's contact points are collected on a single screen. The note "He came to the of fi ce yesterday, we talked about these" is kept digitally even if it is offline.
- **Team Information**: Your team member who will meet with the customer after you will be informed about this important meeting that takes place outside the system.
- **Performance and Tracking**: It allows the efforts of the representative to be visible not only through the system, but also through the field or external channels.

::: tip Professional Tip
After having a face-to-face meeting with the customer or a phone call outside, log in to INTARVAS AIO and create a short "Manual Meeting" log immediately. Forgotten details can lead to operational errors in the future.
:::
