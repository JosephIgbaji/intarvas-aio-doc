Settings is the control center where you determine which information you will see across the platform or on the person card lists in which order. This section allows you to focus by preventing screen clutter.

## 🛠 What Can You Do?

- **Field Hide**: You can hide data fields (e.g. Social Media, Product, etc.) that are not necessary for your work flow or that you do not need at the moment from the list, and make the screen look simpler.
- **Sorting Change**: You can keep the columns you use most often (e.g. Result Code or Reminder) at the top by putting them in the front of your eyes.
- **Personalized Appearance**: By designing your screen according to your own working habits, you can minimize your data search time.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Operational Focus**: It only allows you to see the data you need, prevents your attention from being distracted and increases your processing speed.
- **Screen Efficiency**: Especially on small screen devices, it allows the most critical information to fi t on the screen by hiding unnecessary columns.
- **Quick Action**: Having the data you look at the most in the leftmost column helps you make decisions in seconds.

::: tip Professional Recommendation
You can update the "Settings" section according to your weekly workload. For example, if you are in a busy "Campaign" period that week, you can move the campaign column to the front; if you are in the follow-up week, you can move the "Reminder" column to the top.
:::
