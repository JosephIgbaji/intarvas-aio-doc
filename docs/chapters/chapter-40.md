Access URL; https://app.intarvas.com/wallboard Create Dashboard allows you to design your own "dashboard" to instantly monitor your operational efficiency. Each user or administrator can create a different screen according to their own priorities.

## 🛠 How to Use?

- **Creating a Dashboard**: You can build your own custom dashboard by selecting the data areas, graph types or performance metrics you want to see (e.g. Total call count, pending requests, successful sales).
- **Selective View**: You can create multiple panels (For example: "Sales-Oriented Panel", "Support-Oriented Panel"). From the Panel Selection section, you can instantly activate the dashboard you want to monitor according to your current workload.
- **Flexible Widget Structure**: You can place the areas you add to your panel as you wish, and make the most critical data the largest and most visible.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Real-time Situation Analysis**: You can see the answers to questions such as "How many meetings did we have today?", "How many offers are waiting?" without looking for them in reports, as soon as you open the screen.
- **Data-Driven Decision Making**: Panels allow you to instantly detect bottlenecks in operations (e.g. increasing waiting requests) and take quick action.
- **Personalized Productivity**: While a representative tracks their own daily goals, a manager can use a different panel that will track the overall performance of the entire team.

::: tip Professional Tip
When you start your morning shift, make the first task activating the dashboard suitable for your daily focus point from the Panel Selection section. This will give you a clear vision of what you should focus on throughout the day.
:::
