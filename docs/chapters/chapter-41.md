Representatives is the central management area where you can monitor the current status, workload and activity of all users (customer representatives) defined in the system.

## 🛠 What Can You Track?

- **Instant Status Changes**: You can see in real time which representative is in the "Available", "In Discussion", "Break" or "Busy" mode.
- **Total Number of Users**: You can monitor the total number of personnel registered in the system and how many of these personnel are currently active (online).
- **Operational Balance**: While there are pending calls or correspondence, you can intervene in the work flow by seeing which representatives are in break status.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Efficient Resource Management**: It allows you to direct personnel to the right channels by monitoring team statuses during periods of high call traffic.
- **Performance Monitoring**: It maintains operational discipline by tracking how much time representatives spend in each status during the day (e.g., total break time).
- **Rapid Intervention**: You can detect whether there is a technical malfunction by noticing the staff who should be in the system but do not appear active or remain "In Meeting" for a long time.

::: tip Professional Tip
Keep the "Representatives" screen always open in a corner of your main dashboard. This way, you can keep an eye on your team and prevent operational bottlenecks from forming.
:::
