Components are a performance indicator that shows which communication channels representatives use at what intensity during the day and how many transactions they perform in total.

## 🛠 What Can You Track?

- **Channel-Based Transaction Counts**: You can see data such as how many messages were sent via WhatsApp, how many voice calls were answered or how many emails were sent separately.
- **User Performance**: You can analyze which representative is more active on which channel and how the total workload is distributed among the channels.
- **Instant Data**: You can access the numerical breakdown of all interactions that have occurred from the beginning of the day until that moment.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Resource Planning**: If the "WhatsApp" transaction volumes are much higher than the "Voice Call" volumes, you can manage the intensity by shifting a larger portion of your team to the communication channels.
- **Performance Comparison**: You can measure the operational speed by comparing the targeted transaction volumes with the actual volumes.
- **Omnichannel Visibility**: Seeing the performance of all channels in bulk on a single "Components" screen proves how effectively the platform is used.

::: tip Professional Tip
Use the "Components" data as a basis for your daily meetings. Seeing numerically which channel requires more effort is your strongest argument to motivate your team and improve processes.
:::
