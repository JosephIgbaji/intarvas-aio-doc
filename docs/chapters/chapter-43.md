Incoming Calls is the data center where you monitor the performance metrics, density maps and success rates of all calls coming to your central line.

## 🛠 What Can You Analyze?

- **Call Volume**: You can see the total number of calls you received in a certain time period (daily, weekly, monthly).
- **Response Rates**: You can track critical data such as how many calls reach the representative, how many are abandoned while waiting in the queue.
- **Duration Analysis**: You can measure average handle times (AHT) and the average speed of answer (ASA) of how long customers wait to connect to the representative.
- **Time-Based Density**: You can determine which times of the day the call traffic reaches its "peak".

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Shift Planning**: By looking at call statistics, you can assign more representatives during peak hours and organize break plans during off-peak hours.
- **Service Level (SLA) Tracking**: It allows you to objectively measure whether calls are answered within the specified period.
- **Training Needs**: If the speaking times are too long, you can plan trainings to increase the competence of the representatives in the subject.

::: tip Professional Advice
Monitor the "Lost Calls" statistics closely. If this number is increasing, either your team size is insuf fi cient or there is an issue with the representative status management.
:::
