Outgoing Calls is the statistics center where you monitor the results and efficiency of all calls initiated by your representatives (returns, sales calls, information calls, etc.).

## 🛠 What Can You Analyze?

- **Search Volume**: You can see how many outbound searches are made in a certain period of time.
- **Accessibility Rate**: You can track the information of how many of the calls made are answered, how many are busy or unanswered.
- **Representative Effort**: You can measure how much time each representative spends on external calls and how long these calls last on average.
- **Campaign Efficiency**: If external calls are made within a campaign, you can analyze the success rates (conversion to sale/solution) of these calls.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Proactive Tracking**: It allows you to monitor how actively your team reaches customers, not just waiting for incoming calls.
- **Time Management**: It helps you see how efficiently representatives spend their free time with external calls.
- **Return Quality**: It proves how quickly and effectively calls missed or pending requests are returned (Callback performance).

::: tip Professional Recommendation
Examine the "Short Call" rates in the outgoing call statistics. If there are too many short-duration outgoing calls, your representatives may be having difficulty reaching the customer or transferring the call; this indicates a need for training.
:::
