# Chapter 45: 🎧 Those Not Seen (Live Monitoring and Intervention)

  <img src="/images/intarvas IMGs/SECTION 45.png" alt="Those Not Seen (Live Representative Monitoring)" />

The unconnected is the "live operation room" where you can track all representatives actively on the phone call at that moment and the call details in seconds.

## 🛠 Live Tracking and Monitoring Features

- **Time Tracking**: You can instantly see how long each representative has been on the call, and you can notice calls that are unusually long.
- **Queue Information**: In inbound (coming) calls, you can view which department or service queue the customer came from (e.g., "Technical Support", "Sales", "English Line").
- **Call Listening**: You can include the active call in the Transactions menu, listen to the conversation live, and monitor the quality standards.

## 🚀 Interactive Intervention Tools (Supervisor Tools)

- **Whisper**: You can provide verbal feedback to the representative during the call. The customer will never hear you; only the representative will hear your voice. This feature is unique for instantly training and directing the representative in difficult conversations.
- **Conference (Barge-in)**: If necessary, you can participate in the call as a third party and join the meeting via conference so that both the representative and the customer can hear it.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Instant Training**: You prevent a new representative from making mistakes and increase their con fi dence by supporting them with the "whisper" feature during their first calls.
- **Crisis Management**: In a tense meeting, you can involve yourself in the conference at the point where the representative is stuck and solve the problem without escalating it.
- **Quality Control**: Instead of listening to the recordings of calls later, you guarantee customer satisfaction at that moment by intervening in the live stream.

::: tip Professional Tip
When using the "Whisper" feature, give short and clear commands. Remember that the representative is listening to both the customer and you at the same time; instead of forming complex sentences, guide him with spot information such as "Remember the discount rate as 10%".
:::
