The queue is a live screen listing all customers who have reached your central line but have not yet connected to a customer representative, listening to the waiting music.

## 🛠 What Can Be Seen on the Queue Screen?

- **Calling Number**: You can instantly see the identity information (number) of the customer who is currently on the line or waiting.
- **Waiting Time**: You can monitor how many seconds/minutes the customer has been waiting with the precision of a chronometer.
- **Queue Information**: You can prioritize by viewing which department your call is routed to (e.g., "Sales", "Technical Support", "VIP Line").

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Preventing Abandoned Calls**: You can detect calls that reach a critical waiting time (e.g., 20 seconds or more) and redirect their representatives to this queue.
- **Prioritization**: If there is a waiting line in a critical queue (e.g., "Emergency Service Notification"), you can temporarily transfer representatives from other channels to voice calls.
- **Psychological Management**: When representatives see the number of people waiting in line, they gain motivation to end their current meetings more efficiently and quickly.

::: tip Professional Advice
If the waiting time increases on the "Waiting Line" screen, it indicates that it is time to intervene with the "Whisper" feature to the representatives on the "Unseen" screen. Our goal is always to achieve the highest satisfaction with the shortest waiting time.
:::
