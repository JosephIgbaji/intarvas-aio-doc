Telephone IVR is the center where the voice menu that appears before the representative is connected when a customer calls your institution and the keystrokes made in this menu (e.g., "Press 1 for Sales, Press 2 for Support") are managed.

## 🛠 What is Controlled with the IVR Module?

- **Button Analysis**: You can generally control which menus customers use more intensively and which queues they are directed to through which buttons.
- **Preparation Before the Interview**: Before the interview begins, you can see the customer's selections made in the IVR, ensuring that the representative answers the call in a "knowledgeable" manner.
- **Flow Control**: You can optimize the customer experience by detecting blockages or misdirections in the voice response system.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Access to the Right Representative**: Thanks to IVR, the person calling for a technical issue is not transferred to the sales representative; they are automatically transferred to the correct department.
- **Operational Efficiency**: The customer declares what issue they are calling about before connecting to the representative, which shortens the representative's time to enter the topic.
- **Self-Service**: With the information provided via IVR (e.g., "Our working hours..."), customers can fi nd answers to their questions without connecting to a customer representative, which reduces call volume.

::: tip Professional Advice
Regularly check the general statistics of IVR key presses. If the vast majority of your customers use the "Other" option or the "0" (Operator) key, your menu options are not clear enough. Simplifying IVR immediately increases customer satisfaction.
:::
