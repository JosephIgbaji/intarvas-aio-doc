Representative Statistics is a performance mirror that allows you to see the success, workload and development areas of each individual in your team with data.

## 🔍 Filtering and In-Depth Analysis Options

- **User-Based Filtering**: By writing the name of the representative you are looking for, you can access the daily, weekly or monthly performance report of that person directly.
- **Channel-Based Filtering**: Do you want to see only the representative's success on WhatsApp or their performance in voice calls? You can compare by separating the channels.
- **User Group Filtering**: You can analyze which group works more intensively or more efficiently by drawing department-based (e.g., "Sales Team", "Support Team") aggregate reports.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Fair Performance Evaluation**: A data-based reward and feedback process provides your business with not based on rumors.
- **Identifying Development Areas**: You can plan a special training plan for a representative by noticing that he is very fast in correspondence channels (Web Chat) but slow in voice calls.
- **Capacity Planning**: By comparing the statistics of the groups, you scienti fi cally determine which team needs more reinforcement.

::: tip Professional Advice
Use statistics not only as a "control" tool, but also as a "motivation" tool. Create a friendly competitive environment and an organizational success culture by sharing the successes of representatives with the team who have the best statistics.
:::

## 📊 Representative Statistics and Performance Metrics

Representative Statistics is a performance analysis center that converts all the activities of your team within the day into numerical data, providing real-time and historical performance analysis. Each column on this screen offers a critical indicator (KPI) to measure the representative's productivity.

## 📋 Statistical Dictionary and Metric Definitions

Metrik / Alan Tanım ve Operasyonel Karşılığı Temsilci İşlemi gerçekleştiren kullanıcının sistemdeki adını ifade eder. Kullanıcı Grubu Temsilcinin bağlı olduğu departmanı (Örn: Satış, Destek) belirtir. Aktif Kanallar Temsilcinin o an hangi platformlarda (WhatsApp, Telefon vb.) aktif olduğunu gösterir. Cevaplanan Temsilcinin tüm kanallar üzerinden yanıtladığı toplam işlem/çağrı adedidir. Gelen (Inbound) Müşteriden gelen ve temsilci tarafından karşılanan toplam işlem sayısıdır. Giden (Outbound) Temsilcinin başlattığı (arama/mesaj) toplam işlem adedidir. Toplam Görüşme Aktif kanallarda yapılan tüm işlemlerin toplamda harcanan süresini Süresi ifade eder. Ortalama Görüşme Toplam sürenin işlem adedine bölünmesiyle elde edilen verimlilik Süresi skorudur ($AHT$). Durum (Statü) Temsilcinin güncel çalışma halini (Müsait, Mola, Toplantı vb.) ifade eder. Süre Mevcut durumun (statünün) ne kadar süredir devam ettiğini gösterir. Statü değişince sıfırlanır. Sip Durumu Temsilcinin sesli görüşmelerde kullandığı dahili hat numarasını ifade eder.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Instant Transparency**: It answers questions such as "What is everyone's status?", "How long has everyone been taking a break?" in seconds.
- **Efficiency Analysis**: By tracking average call durations ($AHT$), you can monitor how quickly customer issues are resolved.
- **Capacity Control**: You can optimize the representative's workload by monitoring the balance of incoming and outgoing transactions.

::: tip Professional Tip
Especially follow the "Duration" column. If a representative has been in the "In Meeting" status for a signi fi cantly longer period than the average, you may need to support them by switching to the "Not in Meeting" module and using the "Whisper" feature.
:::

## 📊 Representative Statistics (Detailed Performance Pop-up)

Representative Statistics is not just a list; it is a detailed analysis panel that opens when you click on the representative name, revealing that person's entire digital footprint and performance metrics.

## 🔍 What Does the Detailed Pop-up Include?

Bölüm / Başlık Analiz İçeriği ve Operasyonel Veri Zaman Geçmişi Temsilcinin gün boyu hangi statüde (Müsait, Mola, Toplantı, Görüşmede) ne kadar süre kaldığını kronolojik bir akışla gösterir. Kanal Bazlı Temsilcinin kullandığı her bir kanal (WhatsApp, Sesli Çağrı, E-posta vb.) Toplam üzerinden gerçekleştirdiği toplam işlem adetlerini ayrı ayrı listeler. Saatlik İşlem Günün hangi saat aralığında kaç işlem yapıldığını gösterir. Yoğunluk Dağılımı haritasını çıkarmak için kritiktir. Görüşme Süresi Temsilcinin her saat diliminde yaptığı görüşmelerin toplam süresini (Saatlik) vererek, verimliliğin gün içine nasıl yayıldığını ölçer.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **In-depth Time Analysis**: It gives a clear answer to the question "Representative took a 2-hour break today, but which time slots did he use these breaks?"
- **Productivity Trends**: You can determine when the representative is more active or slower during the day (e.g. morning energy vs. evening fatigue).
- **Channel Expertise**: You can create a special work plan for the representative by seeing the success of his transactions on certain channels (e.g. Only WhatsApp) hourly.

::: tip Professional Advice
Before having a "Performance Interview" with a representative, be sure to review this pop-up area. Instead of just looking at the "total numbers", looking at the hourly transaction distribution will tell you a much more transparent story about the representative's work discipline.
:::
