Social Media Statistics is an analysis module that measures the speed, intensity and solution performance of all interactions coming from social media channels such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook.

## 📊 What Can You Follow?

- **Channel-Based Transaction Counts**: You can see numerically how many requests you have received from which social media platform (e.g., Instagram DM vs. WhatsApp).
- **Queued Messages**: You can instantly monitor the number of active "chat" messages waiting to be assigned to the representative on social media channels.
- **Interview Durations**: You can analyze how long the communications take and how long an inquiry is solved on average.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Digital Trend Analysis**: By determining which social platform your customers prefer to reach you the most, you can assign more representatives to that channel.
- **Speed Control**: Social media users expect a "quick response". By monitoring the number of people waiting in line, you have the opportunity to intervene without reducing customer satisfaction.
- **Multi-Channel Productivity**: You can compare the performance difference between voice calls and social media correspondence, and measure your team's written communication skills.

::: tip Professional Advice
If the number of "Waiting in Line" in social media statistics suddenly increases, this is usually the echo of a campaign post or a technical failure in social media. You can instantly manage the crisis by tracking this data simultaneously with the Components.
:::

## 📞 Web Phone (Live Call Control Panel)

Web Phone is a command center that automatically opens in both incoming (inbound) and outgoing (outbound) calls, allowing the representative to manage the call completely.

## 🛠 Call Control and Management Tools

- **Silent (Mute)**: You can turn off your microphone with one click. This feature is used when you need to get information from your teammate while waiting or hide the ambient noise.
- **Hold**: You can put the customer on hold and play them waiting music. It is ideal for taking a professional break during file review or system checks. Transfer to Representative (Transfer): You can redirect the meeting to another team member who is an expert in the subject matter in seconds.
- **Conference (Conference)**: By including a third person (manager or technical expert) in the call, you can hold a three-way meeting without disconnecting the customer from the line.
- **Internal or External Transfer**: You can safely transfer the call not only to an internal number within the system, but also to a mobile phone or an external number if necessary.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Professional Communication**: Using the "Hold" button instead of saying "I'm waiting for you" creates a corporate image.
- **Flexibility**: The ability of the representative to transfer the call to a mobile phone even if he is not at the desk ensures that the operation continues uninterrupted.
- **Fast Solution**: Thanks to conference and transfer features, the customer's problem is solved in a single call without navigating between different units (FCR - First Contact Resolution).

::: tip Professional Advice
Before forwarding a meeting, be sure to use the "Hold for Later" feature and give your colleague you are forwarding a message a brief background on the subject. Avoiding the customer from having to explain their problem twice will directly increase your satisfaction score.
:::
