Access URL; https://app.intarvas.com/dashboard The Representative Panel is a fully customizable monitoring screen where the user can instantly track their own transaction volumes, success rates and workload throughout the day.

## 🛠 Personal Dashboard Management and Features

- **Creating a Custom Dashboard**: The Representative can design a unique visual interface by selecting only the data that is suitable for their own work flow (e.g., "WhatsApp messages I answered today", "My closed requests", "My pending reminders").
- **Dashboard Selection Option**: The user can create multiple dashboards for different focus points (e.g., "Morning Traffic Dashboard" or "End-of-Day Report Dashboard"). From the "Select Dashboard" section, they can activate the most suitable view for their current needs within seconds.
- **Individual Tracking**: Without needing another administrator panel, you can track how close you are to your daily goals numerically and visually (graph/counter).

## 💡

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Self-discipline and Motivation**: The representative gains motivation to improve his performance by seeing the answer to the question "How many calls did I receive today?" instantly.
- **Time Management**: Thanks to the "Pending Tasks" widget it adds to its dashboard, it immediately notices which request is urgent as soon as it opens the screen.
- **Focus**: A simple screen that eliminates unnecessary clutter and only contains the data within the representative's scope of responsibility directly increases processing speed (AHT).

::: tip Professional Advice
When you start working, open the board showing your priority job of the day (e.g. campaign calls) from the "Select Board" section. At the end of the day, switch to the board showing your general transaction habits and analyze your own "End of Day Report" without asking anyone. Representative Panel: Requests are a numerical performance indicator that shows the current status and volume of cases (tickets) created or managed by the user on the system.
:::

## 🛠 Main Indicators Being Followed

- **Daily Request Count**: Indicates the number of new requests opened/created by the representative from the start of his shift until that moment. This is proof of how "active" the day has been.
- **Total Number of Requests**: Refers to the cumulative number of all requests that the representative is responsible for across the system or has created from the past to the present.
- **Status Analysis**: Thanks to these data, the representative can easily establish the balance between pending and completed tasks.

## 💡

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Speed and Efficiency (KPI)**: The representative immediately realizes whether his average is below or above by seeing the number of requests he opens daily.
- **Task Tracking**: It is a reference point for the question "How much of my requests have been completed?"
- **Administrative Visibility**: While the representative sees this data on his own panel, the managers can also correctly analyze the representative's workload capacity (Bandwidth) by looking at this volume.

::: tip Professional Advice
If your daily number of requests is much lower than your number of "Answered Transactions", check the conversion rate of conversations into requests (tickets). Every problem should be crowned with a request record so that the institution's memory remains complete. Representative Panel: The Queueing is an "early warning" widget that shows the instantaneous number of inbound (coming) calls waiting in the queue that have reached the system but have not yet been connected to a representative.
:::

## 🛠 Functional Features

- **Instant Call Volume**: The representative numerically indicates the number of customers waiting in line on his own dashboard.
- **Critical Threshold Tracking**: The representative can analyze on his own how quickly he should end his current meeting (AHT management) by seeing the general queue status.
- **Synchronous Visibility**: By presenting a similar version of the queue data that managers see to the representative, it allows the team to work towards the same goal (reducing waiting time).

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Quick Action**: When the representative sees that the number of calls waiting on the call board has increased, he can postpone his break plan or leave his work on written channels and focus on voice calls.
- **Operational Awareness**: It strengthens the "team spirit" by ensuring that the representative not only controls his own meeting, but also the burden of the entire operation.
- **Service Quality (SLA)**: As the number of people waiting in line increases, the agility of the representatives increases, which directly reduces the waiting time of customers on the phone.

::: tip Professional Advice
Treat the number of queues on your board as a "navigation" tool, not a "stress source". If the number exceeds 5, it is a sign that you need to keep your current conversations more compact and solution-oriented. Representative Panel: It is the basic indicator panel that measures the volume and quality of all voice interactions (Incoming and Outgoing) that the user has made since the start of his shift.
:::

## 🛠 Basic Metrics Within the Widget

Transaction Counts (Incoming/Outgoing): It gives the total number of calls answered (Inbound) and initiated (Outbound) by the representative during the day.

- **Total Talk Time**: Indicates the active time spent on the phone throughout the day. This data shows the representative's "net work effort". Average Talk Time ($AHT$): Calculated by dividing the total time by the number of calls. It determines the representative's average speed to solve a problem.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Time Management**: The representative can analyze on his own whether he is prolonging his meetings too much or passing them too super fi cially by seeing the average speaking time ($AHT$).
- **Balanced Operation**: By looking at the difference between incoming and outgoing call volumes, it sees whether it gives more weight to proactive calls (Outbound) or incoming requests (Inbound).
- **Goal Tracking**: He can track how close he is to the daily goal of "X meetings" from his screen without asking anyone.

::: tip Professional Tip
If your average speaking time ($AHT$) is much higher than the team's overall average, focus on reducing the "gaps" or "hold" times during the interview. This panel is your best compass to make yourself faster and more effective every day than the previous day. Representative Panel: The Interview is a "scoreboard" that instantly presents the total number of transactions the user performs through all channels (Voice, Written, Social Media) from the start of his working hours.
:::

## 🛠 Widget Functions

- **Transaction Volume Tracking**: It allows the representative to see the total number of calls he or she has concluded or contacted during the day in seconds.
- **Channel Independent Data**: It re fl ects the total load of all active dialogues carried out through the system, not just phone calls.
- **Quick Comparison**: The representative sets the working pace by comparing this number with the data of the previous day or his own daily target.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Effort Transparency**: It gives the power to support the sentence "I was very busy today" with a numerical data (For example: "I completed 85 meetings today").
- **Efficiency Awareness**: Seeing this number on the representative's own dashboard helps them maintain work discipline and know where they stand in the operational flow.
- **Visual Motivation**: The re fl ection of each completed interview in the number is a small indicator of success for the representative throughout the day.

::: tip Professional Advice
If you have a high number of "Meetings" but a low number of "Requests", analyze how much of your interactions have turned into a permanent record. Ideally, every quali fi ed meeting should culminate in a request or record. Representative Dashboard: User Status is a personal timeline that allows the representative to instantly monitor the status of the representative throughout the day (Available, Break, Meal, Meeting, Interview, etc.) and the duration spent in each status.
:::

## 🛠 Widget Functions and Tracking Areas

- **Status-Based Duration Measurement**: The user can track how long the current situation (e.g., "Break") has been ongoing like a live timer.
- **Status Change History**: You can see all status changes during the day and the distribution of time intervals between these changes.
- **Personal Time Control**: The representative monitors his own screen without needing an external warning to ensure that he does not exceed the break or training periods defined for him.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Self-Discipline**: The representative adjusts the working pace by seeing the answers to the questions "How long have I been on break?" or "How long have I been available in total today?" instantly.
- **Performance Transparency**: By presenting the same data in the executive reports to the representative, it provides a full transparency and trust environment in performance evaluations.
- **Operational Efficiency**: A representative who tracks status durations acts more consciously to maximize the "Available" duration, which directly contributes to reducing the number of calls waiting in the queue.

::: tip Professional Advice
Make it a habit to make your status changes instantly. A forgotten "Break" status both falsely shows your statistics and increases the waiting time for customers waiting for calls. This panel is your most honest coworker.
:::
