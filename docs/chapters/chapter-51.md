# Chapter 51: 🆘 Get Help (Instant Support and System Synchronization)



Synchronization) Help is an interactive communication area located permanently in the lower left corner of every page of the INTARVAS AIO interface and provides the user with instant technical support and system reference information.

## 🛠 Accessible Features and Information Resources

- **Contact Us**: When you encounter a problem with platform usage or need technical support, you can provide direct access to the INTARVAS AIO support team through this area.
- **System Date and Time**: You can verify the current system date and time, which is critical for the accuracy of operational processes (shift, call queue, break times), from this area.
- **Quick Access**: Regardless of which menu you are in (Dashboard, IVR, Settings, etc.), it offers the opportunity to request assistance without leaving the page.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Operational Continuity**: It prevents the operation from being stopped by receiving support without wasting time in the event of a disruption.
- **Time Synchronization**: Even if the clock on your own computer is incorrect, you can manage your breaks and reports without errors by tracking INTARVAS AIO's central system clock from here.
- **User Experience**: Being available on every page gives con fi dence to the representative in complex transactions; it reinforces the feeling of "support is a click away from where I am stuck".

::: tip Professional Advice
Before reporting an error, be sure to compare your computer clock with the system clock in this area. Also, specifying which menu you clicked on the "Get Help" button in your technical support requests allows our team to diagnose the problem much faster.
:::
