(Gamification and Reward System)

Access URL; https://app.intarvas.com/agent-dashboard The Representative Performance Certi fi cate is an interactive success tracking panel that shows not only that representatives "work" but also that they "compete" and achieve according to the specified goals. 🎮 How Does the System Work?

- **Criteria Determination**: Managers define various performance criteria according to the institution's goals (e.g., "50 successful calls per day", "Customer satisfaction score > 4.5", "Zero delayed requests").
- **Scoring System**: The representative accumulates points based on these criteria for each successful transaction. He can instantly monitor how many points he receives from each criterion on his own report card.
- **Prize Pool**: When the specified score thresholds are reached (e.g., 1000 Points), the system grants the representative the right to win the gift defined by the manager (e.g., "Food voucher", "Extra break time", "Representative of the week title").

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Internal Motivation**: Seeing that the data is used not only for monitoring but also for rewarding increases representative loyalty.
- **Clear Goals**: The Representative sees the answer to the question "What criteria am I considered successful by?" in his report card with clear criteria.
- **Sweet Competition**: It creates a gami fi ed work culture that reduces job stress while increasing performance within the team.

::: tip Professional Advice
Regularly check your scores in the "Performance Report". If your score is low in a criterion, you can improve yourself in that area, both approach the awards and increase your professional competence. Remember, this report is your "success story" in INTARVAS AIO.
:::
