Access URL; https://app.intarvas.com/conversation-dashboard The Meeting Board is a dynamic interface that allows the representative to monitor, manage and prioritize all active communication channels on him from a single screen.

## 🛠 Customization and Tracking Features

- **Multi-Channel Tracking**: The Representative can track all active conversations from different sources such as WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Web Chat simultaneously from this panel.
- **Creating a Custom Dashboard**: The user can design a special layout that displays the data they want to see according to their own working style (e.g., "Pending Replies", "Active Conversations", "Last Logged Out").
- **Select Banner**: You can make an instant transition between different visual layouts prepared for different intensity levels or campaign periods with the "Select Banner" menu.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Concurrency**: While a representative is talking on the phone, it can manage 3-4 different communications simultaneously without errors thanks to this panel.
- **Response Speed**: Visualizing the status of conversations (pending, being written, answered) minimizes customer waiting times.
- **Focus**: By ensuring that the representative only focuses on the tasks on his own, it offers a clean working area free from the general system crowd.

::: tip Professional Tip
Place the "Oldest Pending" criterion in the most visible place when creating your meeting board. Customer loyalty is directly proportional to speed on written channels; this board is your digital speedometer.
:::
