- **Meeting Board**: It is a dynamic data panel that summarizes the volume, speed and waiting status of requests from digital channels such as Social Media Statistics, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook on a representative basis or generally.

## 📊 Critical Indicators (KPI) Observed

- **Transaction Counts**: Refers to the total number of interactions and messages replied to from social media channels within a specific time period.
- **Queued Requests**: Shows the number of active social media conversations that have not yet been assigned to a representative and are "waiting for a response". This is the most important data to understand the system's instantaneous load.
- **Interview Durations**: Measures the active time and resolution speed from the beginning to the end of a digital correspondence.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Speed Management**: Social media users expect "instant" feedback. A representative who sees that the number of items waiting in line has increased can increase the pace of correspondence accordingly.
- **Channel Balance**: It allows the team to channelize its energy in the right direction by seeing which channel (e.g. Instagram DM vs. WhatsApp) is more intensive.
- **Customer Experience (SLA)**: Tracking interview durations proves how quickly the representative resolves the customer's issue, which directly affects the digital brand reputation.

::: tip Professional Advice
If the number of "Waiting in Line" on your chat board suddenly spikes, it may be a sign of a crisis or a new social media post. In this case, you can use the statistics on your board to request support from your manager or temporarily stop your work on other channels. Conversation Panel: Representative Statistics is the control center that allows the user to see all active conversations (telephone, WhatsApp, chat, etc.) he is currently engaged in in a live list and monitor the details of these conversations instantly.
:::

## 🛠 Live Streaming and Tracking Features

- **Active Conversation Selection**: The Representative can select from the active conversations listed on the board and track the duration and status of each one separately.
- **Instant Status Check**: It can see how long each call has been ongoing, which one is waiting for a response, or which one is on hold (hold) in seconds.
- **Focused Performance**: It minimizes processing errors by focusing only on the work flow that "it is responsible for" rather than the overall system intensity.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Multi-tasking**: A representative who manages 3 different communications at the same time ensures that no customer is left unanswered or that the meeting durations are not exceeded thanks to this panel.
- **Prioritization**: You can determine the intervention priority by selecting the more critical among the active tasks on it (e.g., those with an extended duration or VIP label).
- **Transparent Work flow**: Allowing the representative to instantly see the load on themselves, enables them to optimize their working pace according to scienti fi c data.

::: tip Professional Advice
When selecting active conversations on your board, check your controls starting with the one with the longest "Interview Duration". A long active session may indicate that the customer is waiting for a solution or that the representative is stuck at some point.
:::

## 📂 Request Board (Process and Solution Performance)

Access URL; https://app.intarvas.com/ticket-dashboard The Request Board is an in-depth analysis panel that shows the life cycle, resolution speed and current status distributions of the requests (tickets) created by the representative or assigned to him/her.

## 🛠 Critical Metrics and Conditions Being Observed

Request Distribution (Based on Status): It numerically lists how many of the opened requests are in the "New", "Processing", "Pending" or "Resolved" state.

- **Request Processing Time**: The time between the creation of a request and the start of its processing by the representative. It measures the representative's "agility".
- **Request Processing Time**: Refers to the total time actively worked on the request (hours spent) above the request. Average Turnaround Time ($MTTR$): It is the average time taken from the opening of a request to its complete closure. It shows the representative's overall solution performance.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Service Quality (SLA)**: Tracking processing start and end times allows you to prevent "delays" that directly affect customer satisfaction ($CSAT$).
- **Bottleneck Detection**: If the "Processing Time" is too long, it can be determined that the representative needs technical support or additional training in that area.
- **Workload Balance**: Knowing how many requests there are in each situation (e.g., "Waiting for Customer Response") helps determine priorities during the day.

::: tip Professional Advice
To reduce the "Average Turnaround Time" on your board, move requests to the "Processing" status without waiting for them. Remember, a quick initial response increases the customer's patience and con fi dence during the time it takes to resolve the issue.
:::
