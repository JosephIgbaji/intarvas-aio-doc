Access URL; https://app.intarvas.com/contacts All People is the database where all your customers, business partners and contacts registered in the system are viewed, filtered and managed from a single center.

## 🛠 Advanced Data Filtering and Query Features

- **Detailed Search and Filtering**: You can instantly narrow down your contact list; by name, phone number, email, tag, city or according to specially defined criteria.
- **Save Filters**: You can save your frequently used search criteria (e.g., "VIP Customers", "Registrants in the Last 1 Month", "Those with Complaint Tag") by naming them.
- **Registered Inquiry**: By selecting the filters you have saved with one click, you can access the segment you need from the list of thousands of people in seconds.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Segmentation**: You can group your customers and develop personalized strategies for them (e.g. special day celebrations or campaign calls).
- **Data Structure**: The answer to the question "When was the last time you met with whom?" is hidden on the card of each person in this list.
- **Speed and Efficiency**: Instead of creating the same filters repeatedly, you minimize operational time loss by using registered queries.

::: tip Professional Tip
Create a "Requiring Follow-up" filter for yourself in the "All People" field and save this filter. When you start your morning shift, you can instantly focus on the priority customer group you need to deal with by running this registered query.
:::
