Access URL; https://app.intarvas.com/mycontacts My People is a dedicated workspace that lists the people you have been assigned as "owners" among all customers in the system. This module allows you to filter out the general data clutter and focus only on the relationships you manage.

## 🛠 Functional Features and Usage

- **Ownership Filter**: Automatically brings you customer records that have been assigned to you or created by you.
- **Advanced Filtering Power**: You can use all the powerful filtering, saving and cleaning tools you have in the "All People" menu exactly in your own list.
- **Segmentation**: Within your own portfolio, you can also make sub-breakdowns based on criteria such as city, label or transaction history.

## 💡 Why is it Important?

- **Focused Work**: This screen is the answer to the question "Who do I need to pay attention to today?" You focus directly on your own workload without wasting time with other representatives' portfolios.
- **Responsibility Tracking**: Being the "owner" of a customer shows that you are primarily responsible for the quality and continuity of the communication established with that person.
- **Operational Efficiency**: You can manage your sales or support processes much faster by using filters registered in your own portfolio.

::: tip Professional Tip
When starting your shift, first enter the "My Contacts" menu and check if you have a new customer assigned to you. By regularly adding "Reminders" to the people in your own portfolio, you can ensure that none of your contacts remain uninterested.
:::
